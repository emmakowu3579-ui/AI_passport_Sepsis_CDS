Clinical Decision Support Platform

Local RAG-Powered Sepsis Treatment Recommender

A privacy-preserving, point-of-care clinical workstation combining deterministic Sepsis-3 scoring, "What-If" hemodynamic resuscitation simulation, 5-drug organ safety gating, and grounded guideline retrieval.

100% Client-Side Privacy (Zero PHI Egress) Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021 Grounded Deterministic Math (Zero LLM Hallucinations) PhysioNet 2019 ICU Cohort Validated

1. Deterministic SOFA Engine

Sepsis-3 International Consensus

Implements deterministic mathematical scoring across 6 organ systems (Respiration, Coagulation, Liver, Cardiovascular, CNS, and Renal). All calculations execute instantly via certified medical decision rules without non-deterministic LLM arithmetic to guarantee 100% precision and zero arithmetic hallucinations.

  • Automated dual-unit normalization (Lactate in mmol/L and mg/dL).
  • Last-Observation-Carried-Forward (LOCF) stale lab tags (> 6h).
  • Visual breakdown of acute organ failure (≥ 2 points).

2. "What-If" Resuscitation

Hemodynamic Response Simulator

Provides interactive bedside simulation for volume resuscitation and vasopressor titration. Evaluates whether a fluid bolus (0–40 mL/kg) and norepinephrine titration will successfully lift Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) into the adequate perfusion target (≥ 65 mmHg) while assessing pulmonary edema risks in acute respiratory compromise (SaO2/FiO2 < 200).

  • Interactive fluid & vasopressor sensitivity gauges.
  • Continuous MAP 65 target line and danger zone shading.
  • Dynamic volume responsiveness evaluation alerts.

3. 5-Drug Organ Safety Gates

Renal & Hepatic Contraindication Engine

Active rule-based safety gating guards against toxic combinations before ordering. Continuously checks real-time kidney function (eGFR, serum creatinine, urine output) against empiric antimicrobial regimens (Vancomycin, Piperacillin-Tazobactam, Cefepime, Meropenem, Gentamicin).

  • Vancomycin + Pip-Tazo: Synergistic nephrotoxicity detection.
  • Cefepime: Neurotoxicity & encephalopathy blocks in AKI.
  • Gentamicin: Absolute contraindication blocks in renal failure.

4. Local Grounded RAG

Verifiable SSC-2021 Guidance

Synthesizes structured bedside consultation notes grounded in 48 indexed guideline chunks from the Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021. Every recommendation is paired with interactive citation pills ([SSC-2021-...], [DOSE-...]) allowing instant auditing of verbatim guideline text, evidence quality, and recommendation strength.

  • Verifiable verbatim citation inspection drawer.
  • Standardized EHR Consultation Note with one-click export.
  • Multiple local inference modes (Extractive or Local Ollama).

Clinical Evidence Base & Regulatory Guidelines

All physiological criteria, severity staging, fluid recommendations, and antimicrobial dosing intervals incorporated into the CDS engine are derived strictly from published peer-reviewed medical consensus:

Clinical Guideline / Consensus Governing Society Key Medical Recommendations Incorporated Primary Publication Citation
Surviving Sepsis Campaign (SSC 2021) SCCM / ESICM International Consensus 1-hour bundle initiation, ≥ 30 mL/kg balanced crystalloids, norepinephrine as first-line pressor (MAP ≥ 65 mmHg), dynamic fluid responsiveness. Evans L, et al. Crit Care Med. 2021;49(11):e1063–e1143.
Sepsis-3 Consensus Definitions SCCM & ESICM Consensus Task Force Defines sepsis as life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response (ΔSOFA ≥ 2), and septic shock as persistent hypotension requiring vasopressors with lactate > 2 mmol/L. Singer M, et al. JAMA. 2016;315(8):801–810.
KDIGO Clinical Practice Guideline for AKI Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes Staging acute kidney injury by serum creatinine elevation (≥ 0.3 mg/dL or ≥ 1.5× baseline) and hourly urine output reduction (< 0.5 mL/kg/h). KDIGO AKI Work Group. Kidney Int Suppl. 2012;2(1):1–138.
Sanford Guide & IDSA Antimicrobial Guidance IDSA / Sanford Guide Editorial Board Weight-based and CrCl-adjusted dosing for Vancomycin, Cefepime, Piperacillin-Tazobactam, Meropenem, and Gentamicin. Avoidance of synergistic nephrotoxins in critical illness. Gilbert DN, et al. The Sanford Guide to Antimicrobial Therapy. 53rd ed. 2023.

HIPAA & Zero-PHI Client-Side Architecture

Unlike cloud-based AI tools that transmit sensitive patient data to third-party proprietary servers, this application operates under a strict Zero-PHI Egress Posture:

Zero External Transmission: 0 bytes of patient vitals, labs, notes, or identifiers ever leave the local clinician workstation.
In-Browser Vector Search: Guideline retrieval embeddings are indexed and compared directly within browser memory using cosine dot-product ranking.
Local Air-Gapped Inference: Generative consultation synthesis connects exclusively to local loopback endpoints (127.0.0.1:11434 Ollama or WebGPU).
Audit Logging: Clinician actions and clipboard exports are stored solely in local workstation session storage.

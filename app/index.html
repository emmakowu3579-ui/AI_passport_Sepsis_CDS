💡 Clinical Pearls & Critical Traps

  • Acute Change Requirement: Sepsis-3 requires an acute change of ≥ 2 points from baseline. For patients with pre-existing organ dysfunction (e.g., chronic kidney disease), assume baseline is the pre-admission stable state.
  • Vancomycin + Pip-Tazo Nephrotoxicity: Synergistic nephrotoxicity is well-documented. Daily creatinine monitoring is mandatory; de-escalate as soon as culture results permit.
  • Cefepime Neurotoxicity in AKI: Dose must be reduced to 2 g q12h in renal dysfunction to prevent encephalopathy, myoclonus, and nonconvulsive status epilepticus.
  • Dynamic Fluid Responsiveness: Avoid fixed fluid boluses in hypoxemic ARDS. Assess passive leg raise or pulse pressure variation.
